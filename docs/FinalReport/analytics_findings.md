# Analytics Findings And Recommendations

## Visual Summary Inputs
The dashboard is generated from 30 synthetic learner records.

## Five Data-Driven Insights
1. High-risk profile concentration: 10 of 30 learners were flagged as high-risk by attendance and access-confidence rules.
2. Access constraints are significant: 15 learners reported limited or unstable internet.
3. Device limitation pattern: 8 learners are phone-only users and 7 rely on shared laptops.
4. Support demand concentration: Data and transport support categories each represent major portions of need.
5. Foundational confidence gap: 15 learners have low average confidence across digital, programming, and AI familiarity metrics.

## Practical Recommendations
1. Connectivity and device support package
- Action: allocate data vouchers and expand device lending.
- Why: weak internet and phone-only access strongly correlate with risk flags.

2. Targeted attendance support workflow
- Action: assign mentor check-ins for flagged learners and monitor attendance weekly.
- Why: early intervention can reduce dropout risk for high-risk profiles.

3. Confidence-boost academic clinics
- Action: run short practical clinics with peer tutors for low-confidence learners.
- Why: foundational confidence is a common barrier and is trainable with structured support.

## Data Quality Limits
- Dataset is synthetic and used for prototype behavior testing.
- Rule-based risk logic should be calibrated with real programme outcomes before operational use.
