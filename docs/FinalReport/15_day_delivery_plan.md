# 15-Day Complete Project Delivery Plan

## Goal
Deliver a complete Student Support Insights Tool submission with a working prototype, integrated final report, and full evidence set aligned to pass requirements.

## Daily Plan
| Day | Focus | Required Output | Definition Of Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brief and planning setup | Confirm scope, tools, and timeline | Project plan approved and task board created |
| 2 | Context and stakeholders | Problem statement and stakeholder map | Stakeholder needs and influence documented |
| 3 | Current process analysis | As-is process model | Current pain points clearly identified |
| 4 | Future process and value | To-be process and value model | Improvement opportunities and expected value defined |
| 5 | Data preparation | Synthetic dataset and data dictionary | 30 to 50 records and field definitions complete |
| 6 | Exploratory analysis | Initial analysis notes and draft charts | At least three chart concepts and initial insights captured |
| 7 | Prototype architecture | Backend and frontend scaffolding | Application starts locally with base navigation |
| 8 | Data input and validation | Upload or entry flow with validation | Missing, invalid, and duplicate checks working |
| 9 | Dashboard build | Visual analytics screens | Three or more visualisations rendered from dataset |
| 10 | Risk and recommendations | Risk flag and recommendations logic | At least three practical recommendation outputs generated |
| 11 | Ethics and privacy | Ethics section and in-app privacy notice | Bias, consent, and responsible-use coverage completed |
| 12 | Testing and debugging | Test log and bug log updates | Five tests executed and at least two issues fixed |
| 13 | Review and refinement | Peer or stakeholder feedback log | Priority feedback incorporated into prototype and report |
| 14 | Report integration | Near-final integrated report and evidence mapping | All required sections drafted and evidence links mapped |
| 15 | Final quality and submission | Final package ready to submit | Pass checklist complete and repository cleanly organized |

## Final Submission Gate Checklist
- Working prototype runs and demonstrates required features.
- Dataset and data dictionary are complete and accessible.
- Three or more visualisations are present and explained.
- Five or more data insights are documented.
- Three or more recommendations are practical and evidence-based.
- As-is and to-be process models are included.
- Business or value model is included.
- Ethics and privacy review is completed.
- Testing log and bug log are complete.
- Integrated final report is coherent and submission ready.
