## Feedback Summary

The value model clearly shows how learner survey data can be transformed into operational support insight for programme staff. The dashboard concept is useful because it moves the team away from manual spreadsheet review and toward earlier identification of learners who may need support with data, transport, devices, mentoring, or academic confidence.

The reviewer agreed that the strongest value of the tool is its ability to combine data validation, KPI reporting, risk flagging, and practical recommendations in one simple staff-facing workflow.

## Improvement Suggested

The reviewer suggested that the tool must make it very clear that risk scores are not final decisions. Risk flags should be treated as support signals that require human review, because automated labels could unfairly influence how learners are treated.

## Action Taken

The project evidence was updated to strengthen the human-review and responsible-use wording:
- The ethics/privacy panel explains that risk flags are decision-support signals only.
- The ethics review states that learner support decisions must not be fully automated.
- The risk table includes explainable reason codes so staff can understand why a learner is flagged.
- The SFIA AIDE evidence mapping now points to ethics, privacy, consent, bias, harm-prevention, and human-review evidence.
